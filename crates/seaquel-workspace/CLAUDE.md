# seaquel-workspace

Moved from the root `CLAUDE.md`, which has the overview and the crate map. "Above" and "below" may point at sections that now live in another `CLAUDE.md`.

- `seaquel-workspace` — the domain crate, reached as `seaquel_core::domain`; it may not name an engine crate. `edits`: the edit and data-tab wire types (`Edit`, `Change`, `ApplyOutcome`, `TableQuery`, …), `plan_edit`, `plan_sql`, `cast_map`, `classify`, `table_select` and `EditLimits` (above), pinned by the 180 recorded cases in `tests/fixtures/edits` (recorded from the pre-5c TS; `changes.json` lists the 89 that differ on purpose; replayed by `tests/edits_plan.rs`, `seaquel-core/tests/edits.rs` and, for the queue's side, `edit-service/fixtures-replay.svelte.test.ts`). `run`: the run's wire types (`RunParams`, `PageParams`, `RunEvent`, …, TypeScript behind `ts`), `plan`, `history_item` and `RunLimits`, pinned by the 95 recorded cases in `tests/fixtures/run` (recorded from the pre-5b TS runner; `changes.json` lists the 14 that differ on purpose, and the replay in `seaquel-core/tests/run.rs` fails on any other). `connections` (and `connection_string`): the one connection builder (`plan` → `Plan::tunnel` → `Plan::config`) that turns a saved row or a form plus secrets into a `ConnectConfig` and `TunnelConfig`, with phase 5a's quirk fixes. The 160 cases in `tests/fixtures/connect-config-v2` are the spec (README); `changedBy` names the cases that differ on purpose from the old TypeScript builder. Change a v2 case only when the behaviour is meant to change, and say why in its README. `shared` (5e: the five file formats, names, hashes, pairing, `plan_sync`, `plan_publish`, `pick_project_dir`) and `imports` (the TablePlus and DBeaver mapping) are described under Core above; `shared_api` holds their wire answers (`SyncReport`, `UnlinkReport`, `RepoPreview`, `ImportedProjects`, …). `ai` (phase 6) holds the assistant's wire types, since Core has no serde of its own: `ChatParams`, `ChatUserMessage`, `Approval`, `AiDecision`, `AiEvent`, `AiStop`, `GenerateParams`, `SuppliedSecret` and `REPLY_CUT_NOTE`, with hand-written `Debug`.
